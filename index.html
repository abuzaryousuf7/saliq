THE BASICS

What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Understand the technology that is changing our world.

🧠

Definition of AI

Artificial Intelligence is a field of computer science focused on creating systems that can perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making predictions.

⚙️

How Does AI Work?

Many AI systems learn patterns from data using algorithms. They use these patterns to generate responses, recognize objects, make predictions, or perform specific tasks.

🌍

Why Is AI Important?

AI can help people analyze information, support scientific research, improve accessibility, automate tasks, and solve complex problems.
